If you want to show Trustpilot reviews on a WordPress site, you have a few plugins to choose from. They are not the same. Some are built to collect reviews, some only display them, and some need a paid plan before the useful parts work.

We make Reviewkit, so we are not neutral. To keep this fair, we only use facts from each plugin's public listing, we say what each one does well, and we tell you when another option is the better fit. Listings change, so check each one before you buy.

**Key takeaways**

- If you want to **send review invitations** to customers, Trustpilot's own plugin is the right tool. Reviewkit only displays reviews.
- If you want to **display reviews with the least setup**, a plugin that works from your profile URL (Reviewkit or Better Business Reviews) is simpler.
- If you want **many review platforms in one plugin**, Smash Balloon's Reviews Feed covers more than Trustpilot.

## What to look for in a Trustpilot plugin

Before comparing, decide what you need:

1. **Collect or display?** Do you need to ask customers for reviews, or only show the ones you have?
2. **Setup effort.** Do you need an API key or a paid Trustpilot plan first?
3. **Layouts.** Badges for your rating, and grid, list or carousel for review text.
4. **Freshness.** Does it update on its own, or do you have to click a button?
5. **Cost.** What is free, and what needs Pro?
6. **Maintenance.** Is it tested with current WordPress versions?

## The options

### Trustpilot's official plugin

Trustpilot has its own free plugin, also available for WooCommerce. It lets you add TrustBox widgets with drag and drop and send review invitations to customers, including past customers.

**Good for:** stores that want one tool to both invite and display reviews.

**Keep in mind:** Which TrustBox widgets you can use depends on your Trustpilot plan. One reviewer on the plugin directory says the free plugin hides most options behind a paid plan. At the time of writing, its WordPress.org page lists it as tested up to WordPress 6.2, so check the current listing.

### Better Business Reviews

This plugin works from the URL of a Trustpilot business profile, with no API key. It fetches reviews and stores them on your site and supports list, grid and carousel layouts.

**Good for:** simple setups where you want to start with a profile URL.

**Keep in mind:** By default it shows only 3 recent reviews per shortcode, and the free version needs a manual sync to get the latest reviews. Showing all reviews and automatic updates are in the Pro version.

### Smash Balloon Reviews Feed

Reviews Feed is built for many platforms. The free version covers Google and Yelp. Trustpilot, Facebook, WooCommerce and others are in paid plans, along with trust badges and review alerts.

**Good for:** sites that want Google, Trustpilot and other reviews in one plugin from one vendor.

**Keep in mind:** If you only use Trustpilot, you may be paying for platforms you do not need.

### Reviewkit

Reviewkit is built only for Trustpilot. You connect your profile with no API key, and it syncs your reviews and updates them automatically on a schedule. It includes seven business widgets (badges for your rating and review count) and three review layouts (grid, list and carousel). You can filter reviews by star rating or date.

Pro adds a **Widget Builder** for review widgets and **Fetch More Reviews**. You can see what Pro adds in our [Reviewkit Pro guide](/blog/reviewkit-pro-fetch-more-reviews-widget-builder).

**Good for:** sites that only need to display Trustpilot reviews, want quick setup, and care about badges plus review layouts.

**Keep in mind:** Reviewkit does not send review invitations. It is an independent plugin, not affiliated with or endorsed by Trustpilot.

## Which one should you pick?

- **You need to collect more reviews:** Use Trustpilot's own tools for invitations. You can still use a display plugin next to it.
- **You only show Trustpilot and want it live fast:** Reviewkit or Better Business Reviews. Compare their free versions on your own site and look at how each handles review count and syncing.
- **You show reviews from five platforms:** Smash Balloon is worth a look.
- **You want badges and review layouts that match your brand:** Reviewkit, with Pro if you want the Widget Builder.

## A quick test before you choose

Install the free version of your top two choices on a staging site. Check four things: how long setup takes, whether the widgets look right on your theme, whether a new review shows up without any manual work, and how the page scores before and after. Our guide to [review widgets and site speed](/blog/review-widgets-site-speed-core-web-vitals) shows how to run that test.

## Questions to ask any vendor

Whichever plugin you pick, ask these before you rely on it for something important:

- **When was it last tested with the current WordPress version?** The WordPress.org listing shows this.
- **How does it get my reviews?** Does it need an API key or a paid plan on the review platform?
- **What happens when a new review comes in?** Do you have to click a button?
- **How many reviews can it show?** Some plugins show only a few by default.
- **What does support look like?** Read the support forum. Look at how fast the developer replies, and whether problems get fixed.
- **Does it say what it is?** A plugin should be clear about whether it is affiliated with the review platform. Reviewkit states plainly that it is independent and not affiliated with Trustpilot.

## The cost of a plugin is more than its price

A free plugin that needs a paid Trustpilot plan to work is not really free. A cheap plugin that needs manual syncing costs you time every week. A plugin with plenty of features that slows down your site can cost you visitors. Think about the full cost: money, your time and your site speed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use more than one Trustpilot plugin?
You can, but you rarely need to. A common setup is to use Trustpilot's own tools for **collecting** reviews and a separate plugin for **displaying** them in the layouts you want.

### Do any of these plugins collect reviews for me?
Trustpilot's own plugin can send review invitations. Reviewkit, Better Business Reviews and Smash Balloon's Reviews Feed focus on displaying.

### Which one is best for WooCommerce?
It depends on your goal. For collecting reviews from past buyers, use Trustpilot's WooCommerce integration. For displaying a trust badge on product pages, see our [WooCommerce guide](/blog/trustpilot-reviews-on-woocommerce).
